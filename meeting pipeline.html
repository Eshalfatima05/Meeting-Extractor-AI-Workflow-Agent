Why this problem?
Every knowledge worker spends hours in meetings, but the real time sink is the aftermath: reconstructing who said what, what was decided, and who's responsible by when. Teams use dedicated notetakers, spend 15+ minutes writing summaries, and still end up with vague items that die in Slack. This agent addresses that gap — not as a glorified summarizer, but as a structured reasoning layer that understands the shape of workplace conversations.
Who is the user?
Primary: Team leads and PMs running recurring syncs — standups, sprint reviews, client calls. They want structured output they can paste into Jira, Notion, or email without editing.

Secondary: Notetakers or EAs turning raw Zoom/Teams transcripts into action digests before COB. The agent handles 80% of the grunt work; they review escalations.
Architecture
1. Entity resolution — maps pronouns and team references to canonical names
2. Priority scoring — infers urgency from language cues ("by EOW", "blocker", "critical")
3. Classification — decision vs. action item vs. open question
4. Deadline inference — converts "next week" → absolute dates
5. Confidence scoring — flags ambiguous items for human review rather than guessing
Escalation logic
Items with no clear owner, contradictory decisions, or extraction confidence <65% are escalated to a human reviewer — never silently committed. Knowing when to hand off is the 20% a human must own.
